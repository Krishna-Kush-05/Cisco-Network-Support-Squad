CASE_ID: case_01
TOPOLOGY: topo_A
FAULT_TYPE: vlan_mismatch
SYMPTOM: PC0 cannot reach the router gateway or PC1 even though PC0 has a valid IP configuration.
GROUND_TRUTH: Switch port Fa0/1 connected to PC0 is in VLAN 20 instead of VLAN 10, so PC0 is placed in the wrong VLAN.
